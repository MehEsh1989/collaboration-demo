## Creating Your First Image

### Step 1: Start a New Project
- From the dashboard, click **"+ New Creation"**
- Choose canvas size (Square, Landscape, Portrait, or Custom)

### Step 2: Enter Your Prompt
Describe what you want to see:
- Be descriptive: *"A mystical forest at twilight with glowing mushrooms and fairy lights"*
- Include style preferences: *"in the style of Studio Ghibli, watercolor texture"*
- Specify mood/colors: *"calm atmosphere, soft purple and blue tones"*

### Step 3: Select Style (Optional)
Choose from our style library:
- **Classic:** Realistic, oil painting, watercolor, sketch
- **Modern:** Digital art, cyberpunk, minimalist, 3D render
- **Artistic:** Anime, fantasy, steampunk, abstract

### Step 4: Generate
Click **"Generate"** and wait 5-15 seconds for your image to appear.

### Step 5: Refine or Download
- **Remix:** Adjust prompt and generate variations
- **Edit:** Fine-tune with our editing tools
- **Download:** Save as PNG, JPG, or SVG (Premium: transparent background)

## Features Guide

### 🎨 Style Library
Access 20+ artistic styles. Premium users get exclusive access to 15+ additional styles.

### 🔄 Image-to-Image
Upload a reference image and let AI transform it:
1. Click **"Upload Reference"**
2. Add your prompt describing changes
3. Adjust "Style Strength" slider (0-100%)
4. Generate

### ✏️ Inpainting Tool
Edit specific areas of your image:
1. Select **"Edit"** → **"Inpainting"**
2. Brush over area to modify
3. Describe what should appear there
4. Apply changes

### 📁 Collections
Organize your creations:
- Create collections by project or theme
- Share collections with team members
- Export entire collections as ZIP

### 👥 Collaboration
- **Share:** Generate shareable links (view/edit permissions)
- **Comments:** Leave feedback on team members' creations
- **Version History:** Track changes and revert if needed

## Tips & Best Practices

### Writing Effective Prompts
| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|------|---------|
| "A serene Japanese garden with cherry blossoms, koi pond, soft morning light" | "garden" |
| "Cyberpunk city street at night, neon signs, rain reflections, futuristic cars" | "city" |
| "Portrait of a wizard with long gray beard, glowing staff, starry background" | "old man" |

### Structure Your Prompt:
**[Subject] + [Details] + [Style] + [Mood/Lighting] + [Colors]**

Example: *"A dragon // scales shimmering like gems, perched on mountain // fantasy art style // dramatic sunset lighting // rich gold and deep purple hues"*

### Resolution Guide
- **Web/Social Media:** 1024×1024 (square) or 1024×768
- **Print (small):** 2048×2048
- **Print (large):** 4096×4096 (Premium only)

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Image generation fails or times out**
- Check internet connection
- Reduce prompt complexity
- Try during off-peak hours
- Clear browser cache

**Images look blurry or distorted**
- Add more descriptive details
- Avoid conflicting concepts
- Try a different style preset
- Increase resolution setting

**Prompt not generating what I expect**
- Be more specific
- Separate concepts with "//" for clarity
- Check for spelling errors
- Try our "Prompt Helper" tool

**Upload not working**
- File size under 10MB
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP
- Refresh and try again
